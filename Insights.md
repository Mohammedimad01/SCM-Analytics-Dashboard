# Key Business Insights

## 1. Consumer Segment Dominance

The Consumer segment contributed the highest percentage of total orders and generated the maximum profit among all customer segments.

## 2. Shipping Performance

Standard Class shipping accounted for the majority of orders, indicating cost-efficiency preference among customers.

## 3. Regional Trends

Western and Eastern regions showed stronger sales and profitability compared to other regions.

## 4. Profitability

The business maintained an overall profitability of 12.49%, indicating healthy operational performance.

## 5. Customer Contribution

A small set of customers contributed disproportionately high profits, suggesting opportunities for customer retention strategies.
